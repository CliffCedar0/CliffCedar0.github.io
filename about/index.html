Hello, I’m Junjie.

Junjie Chen · CliffCedar0

I’m an MPhil student in the School of Electrical Engineering and Telecommunications (EET) at UNSW Sydney, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and battery health assessment. This site brings together my research, projects, and the process of turning ideas into working tools.

My earlier research spans Chinese topic modeling, topic and sentiment analysis of pandemic news, and financial time-series prediction. I’m now exploring LLM + Agentic AI for Battery Health Monitoring, alongside battery SOH and second-life assessment.

Alongside research, I build interactive web tools and independent applications. I hope this space makes both the work and the ideas behind it easier to explore.
