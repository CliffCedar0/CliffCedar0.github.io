Reading pandemic news through topics and sentiment

Research notes · 2026-10-22 · Junjie Chen

A news collection can tell us both what receives attention and how events are framed. Topic modeling and sentiment analysis offer two complementary ways to examine those questions.

Two views of the same text

Our 2024 paper studies English news about the COVID-19 pandemic in two phases: during and after the pandemic. It follows a predefined news-analysis protocol and uses BERTopic for topic modeling and RoBERTa for sentiment analysis.

The topic model helps identify recurring themes. The sentiment model provides another perspective on the language used around those themes. Together, they support an examination of how news coverage changes across the two phases.

From model output to interpretation

Themes and sentiment labels are useful starting points for analysis. Reading them together with the underlying news remains important to understanding the findings in context.

This project is part of my earlier work in text mining and a foundation for my continuing interest in language models as tools for interpreting complex information.

Publication

Junjie Chen, Weisi Chen, Jiaxin Zheng, and Qifeng Xie. Employing topic and sentiment language models for news analysis on the COVID-19 pandemic. Proceedings of the 2024 International Conference on Intelligent Education and Computer Technology, pp. 340–347.
