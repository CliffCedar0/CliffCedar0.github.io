GLMTopic: bringing LLMs into Chinese topic modeling

Research notes · 2026-10-22 · Junjie Chen

Topic models help turn large text collections into themes that people can inspect. Chinese social media adds a particular challenge: posts can be short, informal, and dependent on context.

The research question

How can large language models help uncover coherent and interpretable topics in Chinese text? Our paper, GLMTopic: A hybrid Chinese topic model leveraging large language models, explores a hybrid approach to that question.

The work sits at the intersection of topic modeling and language-model-based interpretation. Its motivation is to make themes in social media text more useful for content analysis and social science research.

Why this work matters to me

This line of research connects language modeling with a broader question: how can a model help a person understand a complex dataset? That question also informs my current interest in interpreting battery measurements with language models and agent workflows.

This is a brief introduction to the paper, rather than a reproduction report. The full method and experimental results are available in the published article.

Publication

Weisi Chen, Walayat Hussain, and Junjie Chen. Computers, Materials & Continua, 85(1), 1559–1583, 2025.
